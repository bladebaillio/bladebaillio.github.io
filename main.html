Hi, I'm BLADEr.

Once upon a time, I was a young boy who started making little flappy bird games for his siblings using simple drag-and-drop code. Since then, I've moved on from these blocks to create more complex games and projects. I started to love programming so much because it was a way for me to be artistic using something I'm good at: Math! Something that sparked my interest in programming was the fact that real-life creatures and biological structures could be made with math problems. This led me into the world of Procedural Animation! Of course, none of this would matter to me without my family and friends and the joy they have playing my games. I hope that by my creations I can inspire others to find joy in creating art through programming. My other interests are Music, Dancing, Writing, and Hiking.
